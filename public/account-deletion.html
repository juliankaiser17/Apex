Privacy & Compliance

Account & Data Deletion Request

Under Google Play User Data policies and GDPR/CCPA regulations, you have the right to permanently erase your Apex account and all associated personal data.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Immediate Account Revocation: Your session is permanently logged out across all devices.
  • Garage & Cards Purged: All vehicle cards, photos, custom captions, and rarity stamps are permanently removed from our databases.
  • Social Content Erased: Community posts, comments, likes, and direct messages are deleted.
  • Storage Blobs Deleted: Original high-resolution vehicle photos stored on our CDN are deleted within 30 days.
  • Retention Window: Soft-deleted cards in the 3-day recovery window are immediately bypassed and purged.

Self-Service Deletion via Mobile App

If you have the Apex app installed on your device, you can delete your account immediately with one tap:

  • Open Apex $\rightarrow$ Tap the Profile tab
  • Tap the Settings (Gear) icon in the top right corner
  • Scroll down to the Danger Zone and tap Delete Account
  • Confirm deletion to erase your account instantaneously

Web Deletion Request (App Uninstalled)

Request Received! A verification link has been sent to your email address. Click the link within 24 hours to confirm your deletion.
