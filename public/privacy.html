APEX SECURITY & LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 | Version 1.0

1. Overview

Apex ("we," "our," or "us") provides a mobile car-spotting and automotive enthusiast platform. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparent handling of your data. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is processed, and your rights under applicable privacy laws, including the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide and secure Apex services:

  • Account Information: When registering, we collect your email address, display name, and chosen username. Authentication is securely managed through Supabase Auth.
  • Camera & Vehicle Photographs: Photos captured via the in-app scanner are processed to identify the make, model, generation, and specifications of motor vehicles.
  • Location Data (Optional): If permission is granted, approximate or precise location coordinates are used to determine regional vehicle rarity and display local spotting radar. You can choose among three privacy tiers: Exact Pin, 1km Zone (fuzzy offset), or Hidden.
  • Garage & Progression: Your virtual garage of collected vehicles, spots, XP, levels, and achievements are stored in our secure database.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

Apex utilizes server-side Artificial Intelligence to analyze vehicle imagery:

  • Secure Proxy Architecture: Vehicle photos are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to the Apex Backend API (hosted on Vercel). Mobile clients never communicate directly with AI provider APIs or hold private provider credentials.
  • Google Gemini Vision: Image payloads are processed by Google Cloud Generative AI (Gemini) solely for identifying vehicle makes, models, and specifications. Vehicle photos are not used by Apex or Google to train publicly available models or sold to data brokers.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: Apex does not integrate third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or data brokers. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data transmitted between your device and Apex is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Database records are protected with PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring users can only modify their own data. Private API keys and administrative credentials remain strictly isolated on serverless backends.

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have the unconditional right to access, rectify, or permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile → Settings → Delete Account & Personal Data. Confirming deletion immediately purges your profile, garage collection, spotting history, and Supabase authentication identity.
  • Manual Requests: You may also request deletion by contacting us at privacy@apex-spotter.app. Requests will be executed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Apex is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for removal.
